# Judge Demo Script

## 90-second walkthrough

1. Open the app and show the hero section.
2. Click `JUDGE MODE` or `WATCH LIVE DEMO`.
3. Load the fictional electricity disconnection scam example.
4. Explain that the problem is payment scams usually only detected after money is sent.
5. Show the risk card: `HIGH RISK 91/100`.
6. Walk through the red flags: urgency, threat, payment request, suspicious link.
7. Highlight Scam DNA bars.
8. Explain the Scam Story in plain language.
9. Show the Attack Chain timeline.
10. End with the message: `STOP • THINK • VERIFY`.
11. Close by emphasizing that the tool protects the user before the payment happens.

## 3-minute presentation script

"Scam detection usually happens too late. By the time a user realizes it, the money is already gone. PayGuard moves the protection layer to the moment before the payment happens."

"Our product analyzes suspicious payment messages, links, and screenshots and explains why it thinks the request is risky. It is not just a generative chat wrapper. It combines language intelligence, URL analysis, contextual risk analysis, and a transparent rule engine."

"Here is a real payment-moment scenario: an electricity bill message creates urgency, threatens service disconnection, asks for an immediate payment, and pushes the user to an unfamiliar URL. PayGuard flags the request, explains the signals, and recommends safe verification steps."

"The key differentiator is explainability. Judges can see the exact risk factors, the Scam DNA, the claim-versus-evidence mismatch, and the recommended action. That keeps the result trusted, educational, and actionable."

"The prototype works without AI keys too, because the fallback engine keeps the product stable and demonstrable even when external services are unavailable."
